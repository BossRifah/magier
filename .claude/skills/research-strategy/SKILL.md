# Research & Strategy Skill

Conduct structured market research and translate findings into an actionable marketing strategy.

## Workflow

Make a todo list for all the tasks in this workflow and work on them one after another.

### 1. Clarify Scope

Ask the user for the following if not already provided:
- Business or product being researched
- Strategic goal (launch, expansion, repositioning, etc.)
- Target market or geography
- Known competitors (if any)
- Available data or research already done
- Desired output (research report, strategy deck, GTM plan, etc.)

### 2. Market Research

Conduct and document the following:

**Market Sizing:**
- Total Addressable Market (TAM)
- Serviceable Addressable Market (SAM)
- Serviceable Obtainable Market (SOM)
- Growth rate and trends

**Competitive Landscape:**
- Direct and indirect competitors
- Competitor positioning and messaging
- Pricing and packaging
- Strengths and weaknesses (SWOT)
- Market share estimates

**Customer Insights:**
- Primary pain points and unmet needs
- Purchase triggers and barriers
- Sentiment from reviews, forums, social media

**Trend Analysis:**
- Industry shifts and macro trends
- Emerging technologies or behaviors
- Regulatory or economic factors

### 3. Synthesize Findings

- Identify key strategic opportunities and threats
- Map white space in the competitive landscape
- Prioritize market segments by attractiveness and fit

### 4. Build Marketing Strategy

Develop a strategy document covering:

**Positioning:**
- Unique value proposition
- Differentiation from competitors
- Positioning statement template

**Targeting:**
- Priority audience segments (tie back to ICP if available)
- Segment-specific messaging angles

**Go-to-Market Approach:**
- Channels and tactics (organic, paid, partnerships, events, etc.)
- Phased rollout plan (Phase 1: awareness, Phase 2: conversion, etc.)
- KPIs and success metrics per phase

**Budget & Resource Framework:**
- Suggested budget allocation by channel
- Team/resource requirements
- Quick wins vs. long-term plays

### 5. Document Assumptions & Risks

- List key assumptions underlying the strategy
- Identify risks and mitigation approaches
- Flag areas needing further validation

## Output Format

Deliver findings as:
- An executive summary (1 page)
- A detailed research report (structured markdown)
- A strategy brief with actionable recommendations

## Wrap Up

Summarize research and strategy created. Offer to:
- Dive deeper into any specific area
- Feed strategy into content-creation or campaign-presentation skills
- Create a competitive battle card
