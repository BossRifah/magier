## Brand Aesthetic

Magier's visual identity is **clean, modern, and confident** — reflecting the precision of German design culture and the energy of Berlin's startup scene.

**Aesthetic direction:** Minimal but bold. White space is intentional. Typography-driven. Every element earns its place.

## Logo Usage

- Always use the Magier logo with sufficient clear space
- Do not place logo on busy backgrounds
- Dark logo on light backgrounds; light logo on dark backgrounds
- Minimum size: 120px wide (digital)
- Do not stretch, recolor, or add effects to the logo

---

## Imagery Style

- **Photography:** Real people in real work environments. No stock photo clichés. No overly posed shots.
- **Illustration:** Minimal, geometric, purposeful. Avoid cartoon/playful unless it fits the content.
- **Design examples:** Show actual client work prominently. Case study imagery should be specific and impressive.
- **Color grading:** Clean and bright, or sophisticated dark. Avoid heavy filters or saturated HDR looks.

---

## Social Media Visual Standards

### Instagram
- **Grid aesthetic:** Consistent color palette, alternating between design showcases and brand messaging
- **Post types:** Portfolio showcases, client results, behind-the-scenes, educational carousels
- **Text on image:** Minimal — let the design speak
- **Aspect ratio:** 1:1 for feed posts, 9:16 for stories/reels

### LinkedIn
- **Style:** Professional but not boring. Thought leadership visuals.
- **Text overlays:** Allowed and encouraged for stat callouts
- **Aspect ratio:** 1200x627 (1.91:1) for link posts; 1:1 for native posts

### Ad Creatives
- Benefit-first headline in the top third
- Strong contrast between text and background
- Logo visible but not dominant
- CTA badge or button element in bottom third
- Sizes: 1080x1080, 1080x1350, 1200x628, 300x250

---

## What to Avoid

- Clip art or generic stock imagery
- Drop shadows and gradients unless very subtle
- Comic Sans, Papyrus, or any decorative novelty fonts
- Busy layouts with too many competing elements
- Dark text on dark backgrounds
- More than 3 fonts in a single layout
- Neon colors or rave aesthetic (not our brand)
