# Magier Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)

> This file should be updated by running `/icp-analysis` to build a validated, data-driven ICP. What's below is the initial version based on known business context.

---

## ICP Summary

Magier's ideal customer is a **growth-stage startup or scale-up founder, head of marketing, or operations lead** at a B2B or B2C company who needs **consistent, high-quality design output** but doesn't want the cost or complexity of hiring an in-house designer or managing an agency.

They care about speed, quality, and predictability — and they're willing to pay a flat monthly fee to make design a solved problem.

---

## Primary Persona: "The Stretched Founder / Marketing Lead"

**Name (fictional):** Marco R.
**Role:** Co-founder / Head of Marketing at a Series A startup
**Location:** Berlin, Amsterdam, London, Lisbon, or another European startup hub
**Company Size:** 5–50 employees
**Industry:** SaaS, e-commerce, fintech, marketplace, digital agency

### Demographics
- Age: 28–42
- Education: University-educated, often with some business or design background
- Decision-making authority: Yes — either sole decision-maker or influences the decision

### Goals
- Get beautiful, on-brand design shipped fast without micromanaging
- Make marketing look "funded" even if the team is lean
- Free up their time for strategy and growth — not design reviews

### Pain Points
- Freelancers are unreliable and slow
- Agencies are too expensive and slow
- Hiring in-house is premature and costly
- Current design backlog is holding up campaigns and product launches
- Every design request requires a new brief, a new quote, a new back-and-forth

### What They've Tried
- Canva (too limiting for professional output)
- Upwork / Fiverr (inconsistent quality, hard to manage)
- Local agencies (too slow, too expensive)
- One part-time or freelance designer (good but not enough bandwidth)

### Why They Choose Magier
1. Flat predictable pricing (no per-project surprises)
2. 48-hour turnaround (faster than any alternative)
3. Webflow specialization (they're building on Webflow or want to)
4. No contracts, cancel anytime (low risk to try)
5. Trusted by other startups (Trustpilot reviews)

## Secondary Persona: "The Digital Agency Owner"

**Name (fictional):** Sarah K.
**Role:** Founder of a boutique marketing or digital agency
**Company Size:** 2–20 employees
**Use Case:** White-labeling design output for her clients

### Why They Choose Magier
- Extends their design capacity without hiring
- Flat-fee model makes it easy to mark up for clients
- Webflow work handled at a professional level
- Magier becomes their invisible design team

---

## Negative ICP (Who We Are NOT For)

- **Enterprise companies** with complex legal/procurement requirements and in-house design teams
- **One-time project seekers** who just need one logo and have no ongoing design needs
- **Non-digital businesses** with no web presence or digital marketing
- **Budget-first buyers** who will churn when they see the €2,290/mo price — if price is their first question, they're not the right fit
- **Agencies expecting instant turnaround under 24h** — we promise 48h, not same-day

---

## Qualification Checklist

A prospect is a good fit if they answer YES to 4 or more of:

- [ ] They have ongoing design needs (not a one-time project)
- [ ] Their current design process is creating delays or frustration
- [ ] They have a marketing budget that can accommodate €2,290/mo
- [ ] They care about brand consistency and quality
- [ ] They're building on Webflow or interested in it
- [ ] They have a team of 5+ people (design requests grow with team size)
- [ ] They've already tried and outgrown freelancers or Canva
- [ ] They're open to a subscription vs. project-based model
