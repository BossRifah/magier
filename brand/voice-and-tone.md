# Magier Voice & Tone Guide

## Brand Personality

Magier sounds like **a brilliant creative partner who gets things done** — fast, confident, and refreshingly straightforward. We are not a faceless agency. We are not a cheap freelance platform. We are the senior creative team you always wished you had.

**In three words:** Confident. Clear. Creative.

---

## Voice Characteristics

### 1. Direct, Not Corporate
We cut to the point. No fluff, no jargon, no filler.

- ❌ "We leverage our extensive portfolio of design capabilities to help organizations achieve their brand communication objectives."
- ✅ "Great design, done fast. Unlimited requests, one flat subscription."

### 2. Confident, Not Arrogant
We know we're good. We let the work and the results prove it — not empty claims.

- ❌ "We are the best design agency in the world."
- ✅ "Plancraft saw a 20% conversion rate increase. That's what good design does."

### 3. Human, Not Robotic
We write like real people. Contractions are fine. Short sentences are fine.

- ❌ "Our subscription-based model facilitates unlimited access to design deliverables."
- ✅ "One subscription. Unlimited designs. 48-hour turnaround."

### 4. Specific, Not Vague
Specific numbers, specific outcomes, specific deliverables. Vague promises are for everyone else.

- ❌ "We deliver results quickly."
- ✅ "First designs delivered in 48 hours. Every time."

### 5. European Sensibility
We're Berlin-based. Our aesthetic leans clean, functional, and purposeful — like good German design. No excessive hype. No over-promising.

## Language Rules

### Always Use
- Active voice ("We designed", not "was designed by us")
- Specific numbers (€2,290, 48h, 96+ reviews, 100+ startups)
- Short paragraphs (2–3 lines max for digital content)
- Strong verbs (deliver, ship, build, convert, drive)

### Never Use
- Corporate buzzwords: "leverage," "synergy," "scalable solutions," "value-add"
- Vague superlatives: "the best," "world-class," "cutting-edge" (without proof)
- Passive voice for key claims
- Long blocks of text without visual breaks

### Numbers & Formatting
- Prices: €2,290/mo (not $2290 or €2.290)
- Turnaround: 48h (not "48 hours" in short-form copy; "48 hours" is fine in long-form)
- Reviews: 96+ reviews, 5 stars (always include both)

## CTA Language

Strong CTAs we use:
- "Start your subscription"
- "Book a free call"
- "See our work"
- "Get your first design in 48h"
- "View case studies"

Weak CTAs we avoid:
- "Learn more"
- "Click here"
- "Submit"
- "Contact us" (too vague — be specific about what happens next)
