# Content SOP — Standard Operating Procedure

## Purpose

This SOP defines how Magier creates all marketing content. Claude must follow these standards for every content task.

---

## Pre-Writing Checklist

Before writing any content:

- [ ] Read `brand/brand-overview.md` — know what we sell and our key facts
- [ ] Read `brand/voice-and-tone.md` — match the voice
- [ ] Read `brand/icp.md` — know who we're writing for
- [ ] Identify the content type (blog, email, social, ad, landing page)
- [ ] Identify the funnel stage (TOFU = awareness, MOFU = consideration, BOFU = decision)
- [ ] Identify the primary goal (traffic, leads, signups, engagement)

---

## Content Pillars

All Magier content falls into one of these pillars:

### Pillar 1: Education & Thought Leadership
*"We know design and marketing better than anyone"*
- Topics: Design trends, marketing effectiveness, Webflow tips, startup branding
- Goal: Build trust and authority
- Funnel stage: TOFU
- Formats: Blog, LinkedIn posts, newsletters

### Pillar 2: Social Proof & Results
*"Our work produces real, measurable results"*
- Topics: Case studies, client testimonials, before/after transformations, stats
- Goal: Reduce purchase hesitation
- Funnel stage: MOFU
- Formats: Case studies, Instagram posts, LinkedIn posts, landing pages

### Pillar 3: Product & Service
*"Here's exactly what you get with a Magier subscription"*
- Topics: How the subscription works, what's included, turnaround process, Webflow services
- Goal: Convert informed prospects
- Funnel stage: BOFU
- Formats: Service pages, email sequences, ads

### Pillar 4: Brand & Culture
*"We're a real team in Berlin who love what we do"*
- Topics: Behind-the-scenes, team stories, design process, Berlin culture
- Goal: Humanize the brand, build connection
- Funnel stage: TOFU / Retention
- Formats: Instagram stories/reels, LinkedIn posts

## Channel-Specific Standards

### Blog Posts
- **Length:** 1,200–2,500 words for SEO posts; 600–1,000 for opinion/thought leadership
- **SEO:** Always include a target keyword in title, H1, first paragraph, and at least 2 H2s
- **Structure:** Problem → Why it matters → Solution → Evidence → CTA
- **CTA:** Every blog post ends with a CTA to book a call or start a subscription
- **Tone:** Educational but confident. Write for a startup founder who is smart but busy.

### LinkedIn Posts
- **Length:** 150–300 words. Short paragraphs. White space is key.
- **Hook:** First line must stop the scroll — bold claim, surprising stat, or relatable frustration
- **Format options:** Single insight, short story, list (numbered), or hot take
- **CTA:** Ask a question, invite comments, or link to a blog/case study
- **Posting frequency:** 3–5x per week

### Instagram
- **Caption length:** 50–150 words for feed posts
- **Hook:** First line visible before "more" — make it count
- **Hashtags:** 5–10 relevant hashtags (mix of popular and niche)
- **Visual:** Always reference `visual-identity.md` for style direction
- **Content mix:** 40% portfolio/results, 30% education, 20% brand, 10% promotional

### Email
- **Subject line:** 35–55 characters. Benefit-first or curiosity-driven. A/B test variants.
- **Preview text:** Complement the subject line, don't repeat it.
- **Body:** One main point per email. Short paragraphs.
- **CTA:** One primary CTA per email. Use a button, not just a text link.
- **Signature:** Always include — name, role, link to book a call

### Ad Copy (Paid Social / Search)
- **Headline (social):** Max 40 characters. Lead with the benefit or the pain point.
- **Body (social):** 125 characters for primary text (more can be shown but may be cut off)
- **Headline (search):** 30 characters per headline (3 headlines recommended)
- **Description (search):** 90 characters per description
- **Always create:** 3 headline variants + 2 body variants for A/B testing

## Quality Standards

Every piece of content must:
1. Speak directly to the ICP (see `brand/icp.md`)
2. Match the voice and tone (see `brand/voice-and-tone.md`)
3. Include at least one specific fact, stat, or proof point
4. Have a clear, actionable CTA
5. Be free of corporate jargon and filler phrases

---

## File Naming Convention

Save content outputs to the correct folder with this naming convention:

```
content/[channel]/[date]-[type]-[topic].md

Examples:
content/blog/2026-03-01-blog-webflow-conversion-tips.md
content/linkedin/2026-03-01-linkedin-post-design-subscription-vs-agency.md
content/email/2026-03-01-email-sequence-onboarding-01.md
content/instagram/2026-03-01-instagram-10-posts-march-campaign.md
```
